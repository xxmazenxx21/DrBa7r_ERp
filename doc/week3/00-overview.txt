ERP API - Week 3 Overview (Sales + stock control)
=================================================

>>> Frontend: read doc/FRONTEND_WEEK3_READ_FIRST.txt first (everything new or

Week 3 adds SALES (المبيعات) and STOCK CONTROL between warehouses:
sales quotations, sales invoices, sales returns, stock transfers and stock
counts. They mirror the Week 2 purchase documents, but the stock goes the
other way. Everything in doc/week1/00-overview.txt and doc/week2/00-overview.txt
still applies (login, SUPERADMIN only, { message, data }, errors with
"code" + "field", soft delete, pagination, the server computes totals).

Base URL
--------
http://localhost:3000   (the backend runs on your machine with Docker, see README.md)


The Week 3 story
----------------
The Maadi warehouse (مخزن المعادي) starts with 218 brushes at 10.6818 (the end
of Week 2). We sell 5 cartons to "صيدلية الشفاء" on credit, the customer
returns 1 carton, we transfer 2 cartons to the Nasr City warehouse (مخزن مدينة
نصر), and a stock count finds 1 brush missing in Maadi:

    Maadi:     218 - 60 + 12 - 24 - 1 = 145
    Nasr City:   0 + 24              =  24

  Sales Quotation      -->  Sales Invoice          -->  Sales Return
  عرض سعر بيع               فاتورة بيع                  مرتجع بيع
  no effect                 post: stock goes DOWN       post: stock goes UP

  Stock Transfer (تحويل مخزني)            Stock Count (جرد)
  takes out of one warehouse and          adjusts the stock to what was
  puts into the other                     counted (a movement for the difference)


The 6 decisions (taken; they don't change without asking the owner)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Transfer between warehouses: ONE step. Posting takes the goods out of the
     first warehouse and puts them into the second in the same transaction.
     There is no "in transit" state.
  2. Sales return: MUST be linked to a POSTED sales invoice (salesInvoiceId is
     required). The goods come back into stock at the original sale cost.
  3. Stock count: DRAFT -> POSTED only. No cancel: a mistake is fixed with a
     new count.
  4. Sales price: the user sends unitPrice; the frontend suggests it from the
     product. No price level per customer.
  5. Selling below cost: allowed, with no warning.
  6. Credit limit (creditLimit): NOT applied until Week 4.


The 5 rules of the week
-----------------------
  1. Stock is always counted in pieces (the primary unit). Lines are converted
     with the unit factor (carton = 12).
  2. Only the server's stock service writes stock (ProductInventory) and
     stock movements (StockMovement).
  3. The server computes all totals. The frontend sends quantities, prices
     and percents only.
  4. A POSTED document is never edited or deleted; it is cancelled with an
     opposite movement. Exception: a stock count has no cancel.
  5. NEW: the cost of a sale never comes from the user. It comes from the
     stock at the moment the goods leave (the average cost then) and is saved
     on the line (stockUnitCost). Anything that comes back into stock
     (invoice cancel, sales return, transfer cancel) comes back at the SAME
     cost it left with.
